Using Lingosaic

Play and sharing

Lingosaic provides word puzzles for personal play. You may share links and spoiler-free result text. There are no accounts, purchases, subscriptions or public leaderboards in this version.

Progress and availability

Saved results belong to the browser and device where you played. Clearing site data removes those results. Puzzle availability and the website may change; keep your own copy of results you want to retain.

External services

Following a social link opens another service, whose own terms and privacy information apply. Nothing is posted automatically.
